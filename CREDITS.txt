LAWN OF THE DEAD  -  ALPHA 0.1

Game by cosTanSin.

Penusbmic's male civilian sprites ("The DARK Series", Civilian Pack 1) are the
neighbours in the intro and ending and three of the shopkeepers; the kid, the
pet zombie and Timber Ted are built on their walk cycle. The hit, electric and
poison effects come from Penusbmic's FX pack (also "The DARK Series"). Used
under their licence: https://penusbmic.itch.io
